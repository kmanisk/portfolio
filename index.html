PilgrimageI · Arrival
The first step

Into the dim world.

A lone wanderer enters a landscape almost without light. Ruins sit at the edge of vision while something enormous watches from the dark.

Revelation

Gold against the night.

The darkness opens into a colossal luminous tree. The traveler becomes tiny by comparison, turning scroll into a reveal of impossible scale.

Arcane towers

Moonlit academy.

Cold blues, crystalline light and gothic spires shift the journey from divine awe to dangerous scholarship.

Scarlet corruption

The land is sick.

The palette turns violent. Red spores, warped silhouettes and uneven motion make the environment hostile before the enemy even attacks.

Ashen grandeur

A city buried in gold.

The pace slows. Monumental architecture, falling ash and pale gold replace aggression with the weight of irreversible ruin.

Beneath the world

Stars under stone.

The path drops into blackness and then opens into an impossible night sky beneath the earth. Depth becomes the main spectacle.

Beyond the known world

Into the shadow realm.

The final region feels unfamiliar even after everything before it. Shapes are stranger, timing is slower, and landmarks are partially hidden.

The cycle closes
Die. Return. Walk again.

The final darkness visually matches the opening so the experience can loop back into the first scene without feeling like a hard reset.
